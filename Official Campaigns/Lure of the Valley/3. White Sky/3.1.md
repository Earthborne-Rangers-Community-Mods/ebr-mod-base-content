> The paths around White Sky are a mess. Broken branches and fallen trees litter the ground, and long sections of them have been washed away. The village itself was mostly unharmed. A few boats were displaced or lost, and the utility buildings near the shoreline took a beating, but thankfully that’s about it. Everywhere you go, you see villagers hard at work, cleaning up around the lake. If you can spare the time, they would appreciate your help.
