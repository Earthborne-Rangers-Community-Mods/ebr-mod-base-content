If a Ranger draws a challenge card with a <span class="reshuffle"></span> icon, they may choose to place it in front of a different Ranger who has not passed.

**2 or more <span class="mountain"></span>:** You gain flashes of insight into your near future. Scout 6 path cards and gain 1 energy of each aspect.
