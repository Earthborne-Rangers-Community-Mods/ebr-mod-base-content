> As you mount the summit, you take a deep breath and feel a rush of exhilaration. The view from here is unlike any you've seen before. From this vantage point, high above fluffy gray clouds, you can see for miles. To the south, you can barely make out the Terra Lodge and the strange, eerie woods nearby. The mountain ranges further south block your vision of the rest of the Valley. To the north are lands unfamiliar to you. You feel more disconnected from your home than ever before.
