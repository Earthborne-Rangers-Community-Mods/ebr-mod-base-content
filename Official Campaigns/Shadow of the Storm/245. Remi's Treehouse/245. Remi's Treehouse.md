> High above the forest floor, woven throughout the dense canopy of trees, you spot the wooden structure of Remi Byrd's treetop home. Dozens of chirping birds are perched on the treehouse's roof and the myriad birdhouses along the tree trunk. A few wooden bridges lead from the balcony to several smaller structures nearby. You hear the wood creaking as they sway in the gentle breeze. It looks as though Remi has truly made the space his own.
